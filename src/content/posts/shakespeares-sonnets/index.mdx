With a few minutes of practice a day, you can pick up any number of strange and useless skills. Like writing with your spare hand.

While this was a trial more commonly endured by left-handed children in history books, I, overgrown and right-handed, am here to train my lazy left.

By chance, I happened to be in the mood for Shakespeare, who happened to write a training arc's worth of sonnets. They are not all good. Few of them hit like "_Shall I compare thee to a summer's day?_" But one by one, I read them all, and I'd like to show how my writing improved while copying these love poems.

Here is my handwriting from March, 17, 2026, the start of this project.
